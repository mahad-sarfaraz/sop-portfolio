Stealth UAV with Patent Grant

Full development cycle from conceptual design through fabrication, systems integration, and flight testing. Patent granted by the Indian Patent Office in 2026.

Patent

  • Indian Patent Grant, 2026: Fixed Wing Aircraft, Indian Patent Office. Covers the stealth UAV design including aerodynamic shaping, solar integration, and launcher deployment system.

Key Achievements

  • Achieved L/D ratio of 13 at alpha = 8 degrees, validated through iterative flight testing against design requirements.
  • Led a four-member team through the full engineering development cycle covering conceptual design, aerodynamic optimization, fabrication, systems integration, and flight testing.
  • Designed aerodynamic shaping to reduce radar cross-section while maintaining aerodynamic efficiency targets.
  • Integrated dual flight modes through solar panel fuselage integration, providing supplementary electrical power during flight.

Systems

  • Precision payload delivery mechanism with controlled release for accurate deployment in diverse mission environments.
  • Custom-built launcher system designed for controlled ejection and repeatable deployment.
  • Onboard WiFi camera for real-time video streaming and remote observation.
  • Bluetooth tracking system for accurate location tracking of the UAV and deployed payloads.

Tools

  • OpenVSP and XFLR5 for aerodynamic optimization and performance analysis
  • SolidWorks for structural design and component modeling
  • Flight testing for performance validation against design targets
