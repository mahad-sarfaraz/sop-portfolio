Structural Design, FEA and Fabrication

Designed, analyzed, and fabricated structural components for a 15-lb competition aircraft at Concordia University. Competed at SAE Aero Design East 2026, placing 7th in Regular class out of 30 teams.

Empennage Design

  • Designed and detailed the complete empennage assembly including vertical stabilizers, rudders, and horizontal stabilizers in SolidWorks, generating manufacturing drawings for balsa, 3D-printed, and laser-cut structural components.
  • Analyzed airfoil performance across the flight Reynolds regime to inform tail surface sizing and control surface geometry.
  • Fabricated tail structure components applying lightweight design methodologies, optimizing material selection and geometry to meet structural and weight requirements.

Structural Failure Analysis and Fuselage Redesign

  • Identified a critical structural failure mode in the 2025-26 truss tailboom. Under 332 N bending load, the truss recorded a maximum von Mises stress of 45.26 MPa, exceeding balsa wood yield strength of 20 MPa and indicating structural failure.
  • Independently proposed and analyzed a semi-monocoque hoops and longerons replacement design using SolidWorks FEA simulation.
  • Bending result: New design recorded 13.78 MPa, which is 3.3x lower stress than the truss and safely below yield.
  • Torsional result: Truss failed at 88.53 MPa, exceeding yield by a factor of 4.4. New design recorded 15.09 MPa with a safety factor of 1.33, making it 5.9x more torsionally resistant.
  • Complete fuselage designed at 2309.4mm total length with 18 hoops and 8 longerons. Recommended plywood for hoop material to improve strength-to-weight ratio over balsa.

Landing Gear Development (In Progress)

  • Currently redesigning the landing gear system, proposing a spring and hydraulic shock absorption mechanism to replace the existing fixed gear design.
  • Objective is to improve impact energy absorption on landing and reduce structural load transfer to the fuselage during touchdown.

Tools

  • SolidWorks for CAD modeling, manufacturing drawings, and FEA simulation covering static bending and torsional analysis
  • 3D Printing (FDM) and Laser Cutting for component fabrication
